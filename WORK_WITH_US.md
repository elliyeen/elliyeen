Start with the [three portfolio routes](docs/github-portfolio/README.md) and the [evidence matrix](docs/github-portfolio/evidence-matrix.md), which also lists what the evidence does *not* show.

- **Teams evaluating a pilot** of something I have built. Product pages for Elliyeen LLC live on [elliyeen.com](https://www.elliyeen.com); this profile is the engineering proof, not the marketing site.
